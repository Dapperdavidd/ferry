Privacy Policy

Effective 5 October 2026

What this policy covers

This policy explains how Ferry handles information when you use the Ferry mobile app, website, and related payment services. Ferry is operated by the team responsible for ferry.money.

Information we handle

  • Account information: your public wallet address, Ferry handle, display name, country, home currency, and account preferences, including a referral code or Ferry Miles history.
  • Payment information: transfer and cash-out instructions, amounts, recipients, transaction status, transaction hashes, and limited payout-account details. Bank details you choose to provide are encrypted at rest.
  • Device and service information: push-notification tokens, app version, device and diagnostic information, crash reports, security events, and IP or network information produced when the service is used.
  • Support information: messages and information you provide when requesting help or exercising a privacy right.

Face ID and other biometric data are handled by your device and are not received or stored by Ferry. Your passkey and recovery material remain on your device or with your passkey provider. Ferry stores the public account information needed to provide the service, not your private key.

Public blockchain information

Wallet addresses, token transfers, transaction hashes, amounts, and smart-contract interactions submitted to Monad are public and may be permanently visible. Ferry cannot edit or delete confirmed blockchain records.

How we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts, resolve handles, display balances and activity, prepare and relay transactions, deliver bank payouts, send service notifications, prevent abuse, troubleshoot failures, comply with applicable obligations, and improve reliability. Ferry does not sell personal information or use it for third-party advertising.

When information is shared

Information may be shared only as needed with infrastructure and error-monitoring providers, Monad network and RPC providers, Expo for push notifications, Agora for AUSD-related services, payout partners such as Yellow Card when bank delivery is enabled, and authorities when legally required. Public transaction data is independently available to anyone through the blockchain.

Storage, retention, and security

Ferry uses technical and organisational safeguards appropriate to the information handled, including encrypted device storage and encryption of sensitive payout data at rest. No system is completely secure. We retain information for as long as reasonably needed to provide the service, protect users, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. Public blockchain records cannot be removed.

Your choices and rights

You can disable notifications in the app or device settings, update supported profile information, and request account deletion from Settings. Depending on where you live, you may also request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or objection. Account deletion does not remove public blockchain history or records that must be retained for security or legal reasons.

Children and international use

Ferry is not directed to children under 18. Information may be processed in countries other than the one where you live, subject to appropriate safeguards where required.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the service changes. The effective date above will be revised for material updates. Questions or privacy requests can be sent to privacy@ferry.money.
